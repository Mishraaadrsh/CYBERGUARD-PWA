Core Principles of Personal Data Protection

🔑

Passphrase > Password

Combine 4 random words (e.g., mango-galaxy-river-77). Length beats complexity against brute-force attacks.

🚫

Zero Cleartext Storage

Never store PINs, passwords, or OTP screenshots in WhatsApp, gallery, or basic mobile notes apps.

📲

App-Based TOTP MFA

Always upgrade from SMS OTPs to authenticator apps like Google or Microsoft Authenticator to prevent SIM swap attacks.

🔒

Full-Disk Encryption

Encrypt old laptops and phones before performing a factory reset when reselling or donating hardware.

Actionable Cyber Safety Playbooks

Step-by-step practical countermeasures tailored for everyday digital interactions:

📶 1. Public Wi-Fi Security Protocol

Unsecured Wi-Fi at railway stations, cafes, and airports exposes data packets to Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) snooping.

  • Turn Off Auto-Connect: Prevent your phone from silently connecting to open SSIDs.
  • No Financial Transactions: Never access UPI apps or Net Banking over public Wi-Fi.
  • Use Mobile Data or VPN: Switch to your 4G/5G mobile hotspot for critical tasks.
  • Verify HTTPS: Never enter credentials if the URL bar says "Not Secure" (HTTP).

📱 2. Smartphone Permission Lockdown

Malicious apps routinely abuse accessibility, contact, and SMS permissions to steal OTPs.

  • SMS & Notification Access: Allow SMS permissions ONLY for default dialer and messaging apps.
  • Microphone & Camera: Set permissions to "Allow only while using the app".
  • Auto-Revoke Permissions: Enable automatic permission revocation for apps untouched in 30 days.
  • Disable Unknown Sources: Never install APK files received on WhatsApp or Telegram.

🎣 3. Identifying Phishing & Urgency Scams

Attackers manufacture artificial urgency ("Account will be blocked in 2 hours") to trigger quick panic.

  • Inspect the Domain: Look for typos (e.g., sbi-verify.com instead of sbi.co.in).
  • Never Click SMS Links: Banks and service providers never send links to update KYC.
  • Check Truecaller / Spam: Verify unknown numbers before engaging in conversations.
  • Independently Call Support: Open the official app or website directly to verify issues.

💻 4. Device Sanitization Before Reselling

Simple "Delete" or "Format" leaves raw hard drive and flash storage data recoverable by standard tools.

  • Android/iOS: Ensure device encryption is ON, sign out of Google/iCloud, then perform Factory Reset.
  • Windows PC: Turn on BitLocker to encrypt drive, then format and reinstall OS.
  • External Hard Drives: Use secure wipe tools (e.g., DBAN or Eraser with 3-pass overwrite).
  • Remove Physical SIM & SD Cards: Always check micro-SD slots before handing over devices.

🚨 Immediate Cyber Fraud Incident Response

If you or someone in your community has fallen victim to digital theft or credential compromise, act immediately:

National Cyber Crime Helpline: 📞 Dial 1930 (Toll-Free 24x7)
National Reporting Portal: 🌐 cybercrime.gov.in
⚠️ The Golden Hour Rule: Reporting financial cyber fraud within 2 hours allows bank nodal officers to freeze recipient transaction trails before funds are cashed out.

Quick Emergency Freeze Checklist

1
Freeze Bank Accounts & UPI: Call your bank's emergency hotline to lock debit cards and disable UPI services instantly.
2
Revoke Active Sessions: Open Google/Apple Account settings and select "Sign out of all other web sessions".
3
Block SIM Card: If you suspect a SIM swap attempt, contact your telecom provider immediately to suspend the SIM.
4
Preserve Evidence: Take screenshots of suspicious transaction IDs, SMS messages, phone numbers, and phishing URLs for police reporting.
